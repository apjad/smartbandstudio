SmartBand Studio

A backing band and looper that follows the chords you play.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Support

SmartBand Studio listens to your guitar, learns the chords of a song part, and plays them back with bass and drums in the style and tempo you choose. A built-in looper records your own playing on top, and up to five song parts can be chained into a full song.

Getting help

For questions, bug reports or feature requests, use the developer contact link on the app’s App Store page. When reporting a problem, please include:

  • which device and iOS version you are on
  • whether you used the built-in microphone or a USB audio interface (and which one)
  • whether you used a MIDI pedal
  • what happened versus what you expected

Getting the best results

  • Use headphones or an audio interface, so the band doesn’t leak back into the microphone while the app learns chords.
  • Strum clearly and hold each chord for its full bar while teaching a part.
  • On the phone speaker, turn on Clearer bass on phone speaker in Settings.

Privacy

SmartBand Studio does not collect or share any personal data.

  • Microphone: audio is analysed live on your device to recognise chords and tempo. Loops you record in the looper are stored only on your device, together with your songs and settings.
  • Exports: a WAV or MIDI file is only created when you tap Export, and only goes where you choose to send it.
  • Songbank: the song list is downloaded from GitHub when you open the import screen. No information about you is sent along; as with any web request, GitHub can see your IP address.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
